# Code of conduct

VaultReader is a small project maintained in spare time. This page says how people are expected to treat each other here and what happens when they don't. It covers issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages and anything else in this repository, and it applies to everyone taking part, the maintainer included.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most people asking questions or sending patches are doing it in their spare time too.
- Keep discussion about the work: the code, the docs, the behaviour. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with slow replies; there is no SLA.
- A "no" to a feature or pull request is about the project's scope, not about you.

## Not OK

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Remarks or jokes that demean anyone for their gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, age, appearance or any other personal trait.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information (address, the real identity behind a handle, private messages) without their permission.
- Spam, including promotional posts and generated issues or pull requests the poster has not read and checked.
- Continuing a topic after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

GitHub has no private messages, so reports go through the one private channel this repository has: the **Security** tab, then **Advisories**, then **Report a vulnerability**. Start the title with `Conduct:` and include links to the issues or comments involved. Only the maintainer can see it, and who reported is not shared without your consent.

If the report is about the maintainer, or the content breaks GitHub's own rules, use **Report content** in the `…` menu of the comment or issue instead; that goes to GitHub staff.

## What happens

Depending on how serious it is, and usually in this order:

1. A note asking for the behaviour to stop.
2. Editing, hiding or deleting the content, or locking the thread.
3. Blocking the account from the repository, for a while or for good.

Threats, doxxing and sustained harassment can go straight to a block.

See also [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to contribute and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for reporting vulnerabilities.
